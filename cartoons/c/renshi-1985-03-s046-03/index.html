无题（机构）
Untitled (“Agencies”)

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1985 no. 3 (whole no. 3) · p. 44

Text in the drawing
机构 / 工作
Agencies / Work
Description
A tall column of six domed covers hangs one above another, the top one labeled “agencies”; under the lowest sits a small desk labeled “work,” dripping. The right edge of the drawing is cut off in the scan.
Note
Layer upon layer of agencies over a little actual work. The cartoon is incomplete: the right side of the page was not captured in the handheld photograph, so its title and artist are unknown.
Artist
Unattributed 佚名
Published in
《人事》1985年第3期（总第3期）, p. 44
Themes
Overstaffing and overlapping agencies · Bureaucratism
