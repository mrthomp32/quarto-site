无题（招聘／人才积压）
Untitled (“Recruiting” / “Backlog of talent”)

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1985 no. 3 (whole no. 3) · p. 44

Text in the drawing
××某单位 / 招聘 / 人才积压
A certain unit / Recruiting / Backlog of talent
Description
An old-fashioned bird trap, shown twice. Above, a basket labeled 某单位 (a certain unit) is propped up on a 招聘 (recruiting) sign tied to a pull-string. Nothing comes near it but a fly. Below, a sprung basket labeled 人才积压 (backlog of talent) is packed with trapped birds. Talent that one unit has caught cannot get out again, so nobody walks into the next trap. The right edge of the drawing is cut off in the scan.
Note
人才积压, “talent piled up,” described trained people left idle in one unit and unable to transfer out, while other places went short. The joke turns on the basket-and-stick bird trap: once a unit has caught talent it keeps it, so a new recruiting drive attracts nobody. The drawing is incomplete and no title survives.
