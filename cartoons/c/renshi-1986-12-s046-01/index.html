合影
Group Photo

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 12 (whole no. 15) · p. 45

Text in the drawing
陽谷县打虎有功人員合影
Group photo of the personnel of Yanggu county with merit in the tiger hunt
Description
A crowd of officials and hangers-on jostles into a group portrait celebrating the killing of the tiger — Wu Song, who actually killed it, is not in the picture.
Note
Yanggu county is where Wu Song killed the tiger at Jingyang Ridge in Water Margin.
Artist
Yan Pingya 严平亚
Published in
《人事》1986年第12期（总第15期）, p. 45
Themes
Bureaucratism · Fable, legend and history
