风平浪静
Calm Seas

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 11 (whole no. 26) · p. 46

Text in the drawing
民主生活会
Democratic life meeting
Description
A boat labeled “democratic life meeting” floats on a perfectly flat sea, its four passengers comfortably reading and smoking.
Note
民主生活会, “democratic life meetings,” were sessions where Party leaders were supposed to criticize themselves and each other; 风平浪静, “wind calm, waves still,” suggests nobody said anything critical.
Artist
Wang Lijia 王力加
Published in
《人事》1987年第11期（总第26期）, p. 46
