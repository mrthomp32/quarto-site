无题
Untitled (“Catching mice isn't in the job responsibility system”)

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1988 no. 8 (whole no. 35) · p. 37

Caption
岗位责任制里没有打老鼠这项。
Catching mice isn't in the post responsibility system.
Text in the drawing
岗位责任制
Post responsibility system
Description
Three office workers sit beneath a posted list of their job responsibilities — reading the paper, sipping tea, smoking — while mice frolic on the floor under the desk. One points to the list: catching mice isn't on it.
Note
岗位责任制, the “post responsibility system,” assigned written duties to each job; here it becomes an excuse to do nothing not listed.
Artist
Sun Xiaoming 孙小鸣
Published in
《人事》1988年第8期（总第35期）, p. 37
Themes
Passing the buck · Formalism · Paperwork and documents
