划圈（插图）
Drawing Circles (inset illustration)

Illustration · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 6 (whole no. 9) · p. 48

Passage illustrated
陈科长坐到椅子上，喷了几口烟圈，操起红沾水笔，在刘科长与辛副厂长的名字右边，划了两个大“×”。“看来这圈越来越不好划，科长也越来越难‘当’了。”陈科长觉得转椅坐得也不来劲了。
Section Chief Chen sat back down, blew a few smoke rings, took up his red dip pen and drew two big crosses beside the names of Section Chief Liu and Deputy Director Xin. “It seems these circles are getting harder and harder to draw — and being a section chief harder and harder too.” Even his swivel chair no longer felt comfortable.

Closing lines of the short story 划圈 (“Drawing Circles”) by Wang Jianping, printed beside the drawing. 汪建平《划圈》（小说）结尾，印在插图左侧

Description
Inset illustration for the story: the section chief, eyes screwed shut behind his glasses, holds his pen over the list with a mug of coffee at his elbow.
