只会指手划脚
All He Can Do Is Point and Gesture

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 4 (whole no. 7) · p. 32

Text in the drawing
只会指手划脚！
All he can do is point and gesture!
Description
A conductor waves a baton at an orchestra whose players grumble that he only knows how to gesture.
