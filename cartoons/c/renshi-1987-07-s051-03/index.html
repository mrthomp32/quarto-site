第四个和尚
The Fourth Monk

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 7 (whole no. 22) · inside back cover

Caption
——你就在这儿工作吧！
—You can work here!
Description
An official, his arm around a new monk, presents him to three monks already sitting idle beside their water buckets: you can work here!
Note
The proverb runs: one monk carries his own water, two monks carry it together, three monks have no water at all. Adding a fourth to an overstaffed unit only makes it worse.
Artist
Wang Letian 王乐天
Published in
《人事》1987年第7期（总第22期）, inside back cover
Themes
Overstaffing and overlapping agencies · Fable, legend and history · Anniversary drawings
