打入冷宫
Banished to the Cold Palace

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 4 (whole no. 7) · p. 47

Caption
一定给你保存好!
I'll keep it safe for you!
Text in the drawing
意见
Suggestion
Description
An official promises to 'keep it safe' and files a citizen's 意见 (complaint or suggestion) into a refrigerator.
Note
打入冷宫, 'banished to the cold palace', is an idiom for shelving something — originally an emperor's out-of-favour consort.
Artist
Wang Yan 王燕
Published in
《人事》1986年第4期（总第7期）, p. 47
Themes
Complaints and petitions · Paperwork and documents · Bureaucratism
