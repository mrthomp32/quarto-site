“秘办”
“The Secretary's Office”

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1990 no. 8 (whole no. 59) · inside back cover

Text in the drawing
首长办 / 秘办 / 一会儿我就叫他给你批！
Leader's office / Secretary's office / In a minute I'll have him sign off on it for you!
Description
Outside the door to the leader's office, a grinning secretary takes a visitor's bottles of liquor and promises: in a minute I'll have him sign off on it for you.
Note
秘办, “secretary's office,” also reads as “handled in secret.”
Artist
Wang Yan 王燕
Published in
《人事》1990年第8期（总第59期）, inside back cover
Themes
Gifts and banquets · Connections (guanxi) · Paperwork and documents
