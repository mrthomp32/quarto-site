局长要投篮
The Bureau Chief Wants to Shoot a Basket

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 2 (whole no. 17) · inside back cover

Description
On the basketball court, the bureau chief stands atop a tower of chairs held steady by his subordinates and drops the ball neatly into the basket.
