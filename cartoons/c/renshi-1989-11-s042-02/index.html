无题
Untitled (“Atomic nucleus: Wow, faster than my fission!”)

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1989 no. 11 (whole no. 50) · inside back cover

Caption
原子核：好家伙，比我裂变还快！
Atomic nucleus: Wow — they split even faster than I do!
Text in the drawing
××办 (×7)
XX Office (×7)
Description
A startled atomic nucleus watches a burst of office signboards — XX office, XX office, XX office — multiplying faster than nuclear fission.
Artist
Jiang Sizhong 蒋思忠 (supplied)
Published in
《人事》1989年第11期（总第50期）, inside back cover
Themes
Overstaffing and overlapping agencies · Bureaucratism
