About this collection

Inspiration

This work was inspired by Nick Seaver’s considerably more elegant extraction of illustrations in US Government reports on paperwork.

Sources

The items come from scans of 《人事》 (Renshi, Taiyuan, 1985–) in the compiler's research collection. The collection currently covers 45 issues. More issues, and other personnel periodicals, will be added as they are processed if I get the time.

Items

I got tired of curation and ended up letting a vision model do the cropping and processing for me; this inevitably means some items are poorly reproduced. If you want to use these cartoons for research, get in touch with me and I can try to get you something better.

Credits

Every image is credited as it was printed (as marked in the original materials): the artist's name or seal, the issue (with the magazine's whole-number issue count), and the page or cover. Many cartoons were reprinted from other papers and say so (原载, "originally published in"); these name that original source too. Where no name is printed, the item is marked unattributed (佚名). Some titles come from the issue's table of contents because the page itself prints none; this is noted on the item when it happens.

Text and translation

Titles, captions and text inside the drawings are transcribed from the scans and checked against each issue's table of contents. Translations and descriptions are by a large language model (again, I got tired of curating these by hand). A note explains allusions such as Mr Nanguo, Wu Song or Zhuge Liang where the joke depends on them.

Images

Images have been cleaned (paper tone flattened, levels adjusted). "See this page in the issue" shows the whole scanned page with the item outlined.
