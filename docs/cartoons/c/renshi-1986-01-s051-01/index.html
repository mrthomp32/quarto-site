劝告
Advice

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 1 (whole no. 4) · inside back cover

Caption
——武松同志，这老鼠是你大哥养的。
—Comrade Wu Song, this rat belongs to your elder brother.
Description
Wu Song, club raised, is about to deal with a rat; an official stops him: the rat belongs to 'your elder brother'.
Note
Wu Song is the tiger-slaying hero of Water Margin; his elder brother is Wu Dalang. The rat is protected because of whose it is.
Artist
Nie Xuhua 聂旭华
Published in
《人事》1986年第1期（总第4期）, inside back cover
Themes
Connections (guanxi) · Nepotism · Fable, legend and history
