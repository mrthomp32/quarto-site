无题
(Untitled)

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 4 (whole no. 7) · p. 47

Caption
——干嘛老说假话 ——因为你喜欢听
—Why do you always tell lies? —Because you like to hear them.
Text in the drawing
假成绩汇报
False report of achievements
Description
A small man with a placard reading 假成绩汇报 (false report of achievements) faces a large official holding a bouquet. The dialogue: 'Why do you always tell lies?' 'Because you like to hear them.'
Artist
Unattributed 佚名
Published in
《人事》1986年第4期（总第7期）, p. 47
