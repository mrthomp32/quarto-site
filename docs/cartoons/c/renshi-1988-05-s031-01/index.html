秘书小王（续一）
Secretary Xiao Wang (continued, 1)

Picture story (continued) · Renshi (Personnel), 1988 no. 5 (whole no. 32) · p. 30

Text in the drawing
鉴定 勤勤恳恳，任劳任怨，但缺乏见解和独立工作能力。
Evaluation: Diligent and conscientious, works hard without complaint, but lacks views of his own and the ability to work independently.
Panel text
  1. 牛局长对他的批语是：“勤勤恳恳，任劳任怨，但缺乏见解和独立工作能力。”
    2. Bureau Chief Niu's written comment on him read: “Diligent and conscientious, works hard without complaint, but lacks views of his own and the ability to work independently.”
  2. 局里五位局长，还有各科室头头，那个不点头我能办成事，我能“独立”吗？唉，有啥办法，以后改就是了。
    3. Five bureau chiefs, plus the heads of every section and office — if any one of them doesn't nod, can I get anything done? Can I be “independent”? Oh well, what can I do — I'll just change from now on.
  3. 煤校毕业生分配会议上，牛局长严肃强调毕业生要到生产第一线的分配原则，不允许任何人拉关系走后门搞截留。
    4. At the meeting on assigning the coal-school graduates, Bureau Chief Niu sternly stressed the principle that graduates must go to the front line of production; no one was to use connections or the back door to keep graduates back.
Description
Panels 2–4: the bureau chief's written evaluation of Wang; Wang at his desk reflecting that with five bureau chiefs he can't act “independently”; the chief laying down the rules at the graduate-assignment meeting.
Artist
Nai Dong 乃栋 (adapted and drawn)
Published in
《人事》1988年第5期（总第32期）, p. 30
Themes
Paperwork and documents · Bureaucratism · Picture stories (连环画)
