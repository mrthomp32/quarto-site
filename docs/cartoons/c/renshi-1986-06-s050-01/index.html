划圈（题图）
Drawing Circles (header illustration)

Illustration · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 6 (whole no. 9) · p. 48

Passage illustrated
修建科陈科长正坐在他那特制的转椅上，审视着写字台上那张百分之三干部升资调查表。
Section Chief Chen of the Construction and Repairs Section sat in his specially made swivel chair, studying the survey form on his desk for the three-percent cadre pay rise.

Opening sentence of the short story 划圈 (“Drawing Circles”) by Wang Jianping, on the same page. 汪建平《划圈》（小说）开头，同页

Description
Header illustration for the short story 划圈: young men point anxiously while a section chief shrugs beside a wastebasket of papers.
Note
In the story, Section Chief Chen decides who gets a pay rise by drawing circles next to names on a survey form.
Artist
He Fei 和非
Published in
《人事》1986年第6期（总第9期）, p. 48
