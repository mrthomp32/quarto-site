邪念恶行（题图）
Wicked Thoughts and Deeds (header illustration)

Illustration · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 7 (whole no. 10) · p. 44

Text in the drawing
干部档案
Cadre dossier
Passage illustrated
想到那天李彩琴办理调动手续那样简便，王守平产生了伪造证件，由农民转为国家干部的邪念。
Remembering how easily Li Caiqin's transfer papers had gone through, Wang Shouping conceived the wicked idea of forging documents to turn himself from a peasant into a state cadre.

From the report 邪念恶行 (“Wicked Thoughts and Deeds: How Wang Shouping's ‘Cadre Dossier’ Was Forged”) by Jin Zhong and Shi Qing, on the same page. 晋仲、施青《邪念恶行——王守平的“干部档案”是怎样伪造出来的》，同页

Description
Header illustration for a report on a peasant who forged a cadre dossier: a hunched figure clutches a sheet labeled 干部档案.
Note
The 干部档案 (cadre dossier) was the personnel file that followed every cadre and determined status, pay and transfers.
