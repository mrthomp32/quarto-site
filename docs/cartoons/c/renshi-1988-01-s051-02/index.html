天高任鸟飞
The Sky Is High, Let the Bird Fly

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1988 no. 1 (whole no. 28) · inside back cover

Text in the drawing
人才
Talent
Description
A bird labeled “talent” soars into the sky — tethered by a long thread to a man sitting on the ground, who keeps hold of the ball of yarn.
Note
The title is half of a couplet, 海阔凭鱼跃，天高任鸟飞: “the sea is wide for the fish to leap, the sky high for the bird to fly” — freedom to develop. Talent was nominally free to move, but units kept hold of their people.
Artist
Wang Xiaogen 王小根
Published in
《人事》1988年第1期（总第28期）, inside back cover
Themes
Talent and its neglect · Jobs and assignment
