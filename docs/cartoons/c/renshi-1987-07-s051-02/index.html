高高挂起
Hung Up High

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 7 (whole no. 22) · inside back cover

Text in the drawing
人才使用
Use of talent
Description
A basket labeled “use of talent,” with two men peering over its rim, hangs high up on a hook.
Note
高高挂起, “to hang it high,” means to shelve something and leave it untouched.
Artist
Wang Letian 王乐天
Published in
《人事》1987年第7期（总第22期）, inside back cover
Themes
Talent and its neglect · Anniversary drawings
