停职反省
Suspended to Reflect on His Errors

Picture story · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 4 (whole no. 7) · inside back cover

Panel text
  1. 陈秘书就任轻音乐团负责人，新官嘛！上任先烧三把火。他一上班就审新节目轻音乐《绿岛小夜曲》。
    Secretary Chen took up his post as head of the light-music troupe — and a new official, of course, lights three fires on taking office. On his first day he reviewed a new number, the light-music piece “Green Island Serenade.”
  2. 彩排结束了，他要发表一点自己的见解，就算就职演说。
    When the dress rehearsal ended he wanted to offer a few views of his own, by way of an inaugural speech.
  3. 「噢！小提琴手不错，感情奔放，浑身是劲，搞现代化建设就是需要这样。」
    “Oh! The violinist is good — passionate, full of energy. That's exactly what building modernization needs.”
  4. “鼓手嘛！怎么搞的轻飘飘地，半天听不到一声，这哪里象时代的节奏，热烈一些嘛。这样不行，否则停职反省”。
    “As for the drummer — what's this, so light and airy? I could hardly hear a beat. How is that the rhythm of the age? Put some fire into it. This won't do — or it's suspension and self-examination for you.”
  5. 又重新演奏了一遍，鼓手怎么了啦，鼓点这么重，全都乱了。
    They played it through again. What's got into the drummer? The beat is so heavy that everything falls apart.
Description
Five-panel picture story: the newly appointed head of a light-music troupe, out to make his mark, praises the violinist and threatens the drummer with suspension for playing too softly — and on the next run-through the heavy drumming throws everything into chaos.
Note
新官上任三把火, 'a new official lights three fires', describes showy zeal on taking office. 停职反省 is the disciplinary sanction of suspension pending self-criticism.
Artists
Ma Jie 马婕 (drawn); Sun Yanfeng 孙岩峰 (text)
Published in
《人事》1986年第4期（总第7期）, inside back cover
