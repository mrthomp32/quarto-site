无题
Untitled

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 7 (whole no. 10) · p. 48

Text in the drawing
严肃处理
Deal with severely
Description
A hand thrusts a rolled order labeled 严肃处理 (deal with severely) into the frame, while the official below puffs contentedly on his pipe.
Note
严肃处理, “to be dealt with severely,” was the stock instruction written on complaints passed down for action. The reading is from a small, curved label and is not fully certain.
