"慧眼"
"A Discerning Eye"

Picture story · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 3 (whole no. 6) · inside back cover

Panel text
  1. 厂务会上，刘厂长慷慨激昂：「做领导的应具备一双慧眼：谁是千里马，谁是害群之马，必须胸中有数。比如机械车间的张冲，酗酒、闹事，报废了三十根转轴，难道就不能预先……」
    At the factory affairs meeting, Director Liu held forth passionately: “A leader must have discerning eyes: he must know clearly who is a thousand-li horse and who is the black sheep. Take Zhang Chong of the machining shop — drinking, making trouble, he ruined thirty drive shafts. Couldn't we have foreseen…”
  2. 门哧溜一声推开了，「请问，刘厂长在吗？我叫张冲，机械……」
    The door slid open: “Excuse me, is Director Liu here? My name is Zhang Chong, from machin—…”
  3. “你就是张冲呀！”刘厂长严厉地摆了摆手，“现在正谈你的问题，过一会儿再来吧！”
    “So you're Zhang Chong!” Director Liu waved him off sternly. “We are discussing your problem right now. Come back later!”
  4. “大家看见了吧！就是这个张冲，对待工作简直——”厂办王秘书忽然打断他的话：“这不是那个张冲。我见过那一位，是一个矮个儿，三角眼！”
    “You all saw him! That's Zhang Chong — his attitude to work is simply—” Secretary Wang of the factory office suddenly cut in: “That's not the same Zhang Chong. I've seen the other one: short, with narrow triangular eyes!”
  5. “哦！难道有两个张冲？”刘厂长一愣。王秘书接着说：“您没听说过？机械车间俩张冲，一个紧来一个松，一个喝酒睡大觉，一个上班打冲锋。”
    “Oh! There are two Zhang Chongs?” Director Liu was taken aback. Secretary Wang went on: “Haven't you heard? The machining shop has two Zhang Chongs — one keen, one slack; one drinks and sleeps it off, the other charges into every shift.”
  6. 刘厂长感激地看了王秘书一眼：差点冤枉好人！然后环顾会场，加重语气说：“这个张冲就是不一样，浑身都是干劲……”
    Director Liu shot Secretary Wang a grateful look — he had nearly wronged a good man! Then, surveying the room, he said emphatically: “Now this Zhang Chong is different — full of drive from head to toe…”
  7. 「老刘，老刘！」厂党委书记领着刚才那位扫帚眉小伙子进来：「你看，我给你带来个秀才——市机械进出口公司的工程师张冲……」
    “Lao Liu, Lao Liu!” The factory Party secretary came in leading the bushy-browed young man from before: “Look, I've brought you a scholar — Zhang Chong, engineer with the city machinery import–export company…”
  8. “哦，你……张……第三个张……”刘厂长的“慧眼”顿时缭乱起来了。
    “Oh, you… Zhang… a third Zhang…” Director Liu's “discerning eyes” were suddenly dazzled.
Description
Eight-panel picture story: a factory director proud of his eye for talent denounces a worker named Zhang Chong as a drunk — but there are several Zhang Chongs, and the one he dismissed is the engineer he has been asked to welcome.
Artists
Ye Shijie 野世捷 (drawn); Sun Yanfeng 孙岩峰 (adapted)
Published in
《人事》1986年第3期（总第6期）, inside back cover
