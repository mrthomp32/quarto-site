“垂帘听政”
“Ruling from Behind the Curtain”

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1988 no. 8 (whole no. 35) · p. 37

Text in the drawing
厂长 / 上级主管部门
Factory director / Higher-level supervising department
Description
A factory director sits at his desk on one side of a bead curtain; behind it, at a desk marked “higher-level supervising department,” sits the man who really gives the orders.
Note
垂帘听政, “attending to state affairs from behind the curtain,” described empress dowagers who ruled through a child emperor. Despite the factory-director responsibility system, supervising bureaus still ran enterprises.
Artist
Sun Xiaoming 孙小鸣
Published in
《人事》1988年第8期（总第35期）, p. 37
Themes
Reform and its implementation · Bureaucratism
