心绪（插图）
Agitation (illustration)

Illustration · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 2 (whole no. 17) · p. 47

Passage illustrated
他重又伏在桌子上，用劲地睁大眼睛看起来。可是，见鬼！纸上一行行的字，都交织在一起，不多会儿，又分开来……唉！这是怎么了？他只觉得心绪更加烦乱，便用劲在自己头上有板有眼地捶了几下……
He bent over the desk again and stared hard at the page. But, damn it! The lines of characters on the paper all tangled together, and a moment later came apart again… Ah! What was wrong with him? He felt his thoughts grow ever more tangled, and he beat himself on the head, hard and rhythmically, a few times…

Closing lines of the short story 心绪 (“Agitation”) by Du Shubo, p. 48. 杜曙波《心绪》（小说）结尾，第48页

Description
Illustration for the story: a bespectacled county official at his desk, under a lamp, beats his own head with his fist over the draft in front of him.
Note
In the story, the hard-working education chief Wu Chunzhang is branded “Secretary Lin's man” as soon as his patron is transferred; his colleagues abandon him to court the new secretary's favourites.
