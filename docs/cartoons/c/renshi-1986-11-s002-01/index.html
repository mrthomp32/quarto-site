"考察"
"Inspection"

Picture story · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 11 (whole no. 14) · inside front cover

Panel text
  1. 白局长到某单位考察干部，下属为他摆了一席丰盛的酒宴。
    Bureau Chief Bai came to a work unit to evaluate its cadres, and his subordinates laid on a lavish banquet for him.
  2. 三杯酒下肚，局长开了腔：“我这次是想考察几个进班子的人选，你们这里……”
    Three cups of liquor down, the bureau chief spoke up: “This time I'm looking to evaluate a few candidates for the leadership group. Here at your unit…”
  3. 此时，秦科长见机行事，谎称到服务台找酒而拔腿离去。
    At this, Section Chief Qin seized his chance: claiming he was going to the service desk for more liquor, he got up and left.
  4. 周经理指着秦的背影：“这个人您看怎样？”白局长笑而不语。片刻，他问：“你舍得割爱吗？”
    Manager Zhou pointed at Qin's departing back: “What do you think of this man?” Bureau Chief Bai smiled and said nothing. After a moment he asked: “Could you bear to part with him?”
  5. 秦科长果然得天独厚，连连升迁，飞黄腾达。
    Sure enough, Section Chief Qin was favoured by fortune: promotion followed promotion, and his career soared.
  6. 人们不禁思议：白局长真是不虚此行——不仅发现了发现人才的新途径，而且又“考察”到一位推荐人才的“人才”。
    People could not help reflecting: Bureau Chief Bai's trip had truly been worthwhile — he had not only discovered a new way of discovering talent, but had also “evaluated” a “talent” for recommending talent.
Description
Six-panel picture story: Bureau Chief Bai comes to 'inspect' cadres for promotion and is banqueted; the section chief who slips out to fetch more wine is singled out and rises rapidly.
Note
考察 ('inspection', 'investigation') was the formal stage of cadre evaluation before promotion.
Artists
Li Yunqing 李云庆 (drawn); Qian Ren 黔仁 (adapted)
Published in
《人事》1986年第11期（总第14期）, inside front cover
Themes
Gifts and banquets · Promotion · Picture stories (连环画)
