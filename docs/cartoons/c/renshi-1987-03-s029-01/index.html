机关干部超编及其改革（题图）
Overstaffing in Government Offices and Its Reform (header drawing)

Illustration · Renshi (Personnel), 1987 no. 3 (whole no. 18) · p. 27

Text in the drawing
某机关
A certain government office
Passage illustrated
精简机构，以解决职责不清、机构重叠、人浮于事的问题，是这次改革的目标之一。但是精简机构的效果实在不佳。
Streamlining the administrative structure, to solve the problems of unclear responsibilities, overlapping agencies and more people than work, was one of the goals of this reform. But the results of streamlining have been truly poor.

From the article 机关干部超编及其改革 (“Overstaffing among Government Cadres and Its Reform”) by Yu Zhiguo, on the same page. 余指国《机关干部超编及其改革》，同页

Description
Header drawing for the article: a little building labeled “a certain government office” bulges at the seams, crammed with cadres of every age — an infant, a man in sunglasses, an old man in a cap.
Note
超编, “exceeding the establishment,” means employing more staff than the authorized quota (编制).
Artist
Unattributed 佚名
Published in
《人事》1987年第3期（总第18期）, p. 27
