能人篇
On Capable People

Multi-panel cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 6 (whole no. 9) · p. 44

Text in the drawing
招聘人才 / 没有能人想能人 / 用着能人防能人 / 能人出头怕能人 / 还看得起我吗！
Hiring talent / Without capable people, you long for them / With capable people, you guard against them / When a capable person gets ahead, you fear him / Do you even think much of me?
Description
Three rows of a talent seeker's progress: posting a notice to hire talent, then guarding against the capable person, then fearing his rise.
Artist
Unattributed 佚名
Reprinted from
《讽刺与幽默》 — Fengci yu youmo (Satire and Humor)
Published in
《人事》1986年第6期（总第9期）, p. 44
Themes
Talent and its neglect · Envy of talent
