表率
An Example to Others

Picture story · Renshi (Personnel), 1986 no. 8 (whole no. 11) · inside back cover

Text in the drawing
提倡正气，反对邪气
Uphold rectitude; oppose evil tendencies
Panel text
  1. 刘少奇同志是杰出的老一辈无产阶级革命家。他在日常工作中，为人表率，是我们学习的榜样。
    Comrade Liu Shaoqi was an outstanding proletarian revolutionary of the older generation. In his everyday work he set an example for others, a model for us to learn from.
  2. 一九三九年，少奇同志讲演了《论共产党员的修养》，毛主席阅后批示：「提倡正气，反对邪气」。
    In 1939 Comrade Shaoqi delivered his lectures “How to Be a Good Communist.” After reading them Chairman Mao wrote: “Promote upright conduct, oppose evil tendencies.”
  3. 少奇同志经常深入基层，调查研究，并对基层干部说，你们是社员的勤务员，应该好好为社员办事。
    Comrade Shaoqi often went down to the grass roots to investigate, and told local cadres: you are the servants of the commune members and should serve them well.
  4. 少奇同志十分注重党的干部的培训工作，这是他还经常训马列学院给干部学员讲课。
    Comrade Shaoqi paid close attention to training the Party's cadres; here he is lecturing cadre students at the Marxism-Leninism Institute, as he often did.
  5. 土改时，少奇同志的七哥被划为地主而不服，他亲笔给乡政府写信，秉公办事，不徇私情。
    During land reform Comrade Shaoqi's seventh elder brother was classified as a landlord and protested. Shaoqi wrote to the township government in his own hand: handle it impartially, show no personal favour.
  6. 党号召知识青年上山下乡，少奇同志严于律己，带头把自己的侄孙女送回老家参加农业生产。
    When the Party called on educated youth to go up to the mountains and down to the countryside, Comrade Shaoqi held himself to a strict standard and led the way by sending his own grandniece back to his home village to do farm work.
  7. 每次外出视察，少奇同志总要向工作人员约法三章，不准向人家要东西，不要前呼后拥。
    Every time he went out on an inspection tour, Comrade Shaoqi laid down rules for his staff: no asking people for things, and no entourage crowding before and behind.
Description
Seven-panel picture story on Liu Shaoqi's conduct: refusing to intervene for his brother in land reform, sending his granddaughter to the countryside, forbidding official receptions.
Artists
Li Yunqing 李云庆 (drawn); Wang Bianxiang 王变祥 (adapted)
Published in
