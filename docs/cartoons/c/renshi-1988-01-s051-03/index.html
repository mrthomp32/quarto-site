有一块是从后门来的
One of Them Came In by the Back Door

Cartoon · Renshi (Personnel), 1988 no. 1 (whole no. 28) · inside back cover

Description
A wooden bucket stands in a spreading puddle, leaking through a single bad stave.
Note
走后门, “going through the back door,” means getting something — here a job — through connections. A bucket holds only as much as its weakest stave.
Artist
Wang Xiaogen 王小根
Published in
《人事》1988年第1期（总第28期）, inside back cover
Themes
Connections (guanxi) · Jobs and assignment
